# Privacy Policy

At **Hobi Knows** (accessible from https://hobiknows.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors.

## Information We Collect
We do not collect personal identifiable information directly unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., subscribing to our newsletter or leaving comments).

## Log Files
Hobi Knows follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

## Cookies and Web Beacons
Hobi Knows uses 'cookies' to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

## Google DoubleClick DART Cookie
Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to www.website.com and other sites on the internet.

## Consent
By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
